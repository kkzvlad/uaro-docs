## 🏰 Dungeons

| Change | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Instance Lockouts** | Instance cooldowns now properly lock out accounts upon entry as intended. Instances require **full clears** to unlock subsequent run count up to the limits per week, or you're locked out until reset |
| **Eternal Bastion Exception** | **Eternal Bastion** is the only exception to this due to no loot from the mobs themselves — auto unlock occurs upon completion of floor `100` |
| **Sealed Shrine** | Warping out and vending are now blocked inside the **Sealed Shrine** instance |

!!! warning "Exit Through the End-of-Dungeon NPC"
    To unlock subsequent runs after a full clear, you **must** interact with the end-of-dungeon
    NPCs to exit naturally. There will **not** be manual resets for people who fail to follow
    mechanics.
